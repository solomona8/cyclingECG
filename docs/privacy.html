Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Medical Disclaimer

This app is for informational purposes only and is NOT a medical device. Do not use this app for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for medical advice.

1. What Data We Collect

We collect and process the following data:

  • ECG (electrocardiogram) recordings from Apple Watch
  • Recording timestamps and metadata
  • Calculated heart rate and rhythm metrics
  • Heart rate variability (HRV) measurements
  • ECG waveform characteristics (P wave, QRS, QT intervals)

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, phone)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Any data from other apps

2. How We Use Your Data

Your health data is used exclusively for:

  • Analyzing ECG waveforms and detecting patterns
  • Calculating heart rate metrics and trends
  • Generating analysis reports for your review
  • Creating historical trend charts (stored locally)

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your data with insurance companies
  • Store your data permanently on our servers

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Local Storage (On Your Device):

  • Analysis history for the last 90 days
  • Encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption
  • Stored in app's secure sandbox
  • Protected by iOS security features

Backend Server (Optional):

  • When you request analysis, ECG data is sent to our backend server
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connection
  • Server processes the data and immediately returns results
  • Data retention: 30 days maximum, then automatically deleted
  • You can use a local backend to keep all data on your network

4. Third-Party Services

OpenAI Integration (Optional):

If enabled in backend configuration, anonymized ECG metrics (NOT raw waveforms) may be sent to OpenAI to generate narrative summaries. This feature is:

  • Opt-in only (disabled by default)
  • Configured by backend administrator
  • Does NOT include personally identifiable information
  • Governed by OpenAI's privacy policy

Cloud Hosting:

Our default backend is hosted on Render.com. Data is:

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Stored temporarily in memory during processing
  • Automatically deleted after 30 days
  • Not shared with Render.com for any purpose

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

You have the following rights regarding your data:

Right to Access:

  • View all your stored analysis history in the app
  • Export your data in JSON, CSV, or PDF format

Right to Delete:

  • Swipe to delete individual analysis records
  • Uninstall the app to delete all local data
  • Contact us to delete data from backend servers

Right to Portability:

  • Export your data in machine-readable formats
  • Transfer data to other health applications

Right to Opt-Out:

  • Use local backend to prevent cloud data transmission
  • Disable backend analysis entirely (app still functions)

Right to Object:

  • Revoke HealthKit permissions in iOS Settings
  • Request deletion of all backend data

6. Data Retention

Local Device:

  • Analysis history retained for 90 days
  • Older records automatically deleted
  • Deleted immediately when app is uninstalled

Backend Server:

  • Analysis results stored for maximum 30 days
  • Automatic deletion after retention period
  • You can request immediate deletion by contacting us

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for local data storage
  • TLS 1.3 encryption for network transmission
  • API keys stored in iOS Keychain (not plaintext)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • No debug logging of health data in production

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Apple Watch ECG feature requires users to be 22 years or older in most regions.

9. International Data Transfers

If you use the cloud backend, your data may be processed on servers located in the United States or other countries. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Limited data retention periods
  • Compliance with GDPR and CCPA requirements
  • Option to use local backend for data sovereignty
