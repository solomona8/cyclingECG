Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I get started with ECG Analyzer?

A: You'll need an Apple Watch Series 4 or later with the ECG app. First, record an ECG on your Apple Watch. Then, open ECG Analyzer on your iPhone, grant HealthKit permission, and your ECG recordings will appear in the list. Tap any recording to view details and request analysis.

Q: Why does the first analysis take so long?

A: The default backend is hosted on a free tier service (Render.com) which may have a "cold start" delay of 30-50 seconds for the first request. Subsequent requests are much faster. You can also configure a local backend for instant analysis.

Q: Is my health data safe and private?

A: Yes! Your data is encrypted both on your device (AES-256-GCM) and in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3). Data sent to the backend is automatically deleted after 30 days. You can also use a local backend to keep all data on your own network. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Q: Can I trust the analysis results?

A: ECG Analyzer uses validated signal processing algorithms, but it is NOT a medical device and results should NOT be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider. Results are for informational and educational purposes only.

Q: What export formats are available?

A: You can export ECG data in four formats:

  • JSON: For developers and data scientists
  • CSV: For spreadsheet analysis
  • PDF: For sharing with healthcare providers
  • TXT: Simple text summary

Q: Why can't I see my ECG recordings?

A: Make sure you:

  • Granted HealthKit permission when prompted
  • Have an Apple Watch Series 4 or later
  • Have recorded at least one ECG on your Apple Watch
  • Are using the same Apple ID on both devices

If issues persist, try revoking and re-granting HealthKit permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → ECG Analyzer.

Q: How do I delete my data?

A: You can:

  • Swipe left on individual analysis records to delete them
  • Uninstall the app to delete all local data
  • Email privacy@cyclingecg.app to request deletion of backend data

Q: Can I use my own backend server?

A: Yes! You can configure a custom backend URL in the app settings. You can also run the backend locally on your network. See the GitHub repository for backend setup instructions.

Q: What metrics does the analysis provide?

A: The analysis includes:

  • Heart rate and rhythm classification
  • Heart rate variability (HRV) metrics (SDNN, RMSSD)
  • ECG intervals (PR, QRS, QT, QTc)
  • Waveform characteristics (P wave, QRS complex, T wave)
  • 30-day trend tracking

Q: Is this app approved by the FDA or medical authorities?

A: No. ECG Analyzer is NOT a medical device and is not approved by the FDA or any medical regulatory authority. It is for informational and educational purposes only. Do not use it for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
